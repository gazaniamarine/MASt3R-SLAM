\begin{abstract}
This document specifies the implemented Fact3R-Map pipeline for persistent
entity mapping and open-vocabulary recollection. Complete-frame SAM2 masks are
lifted through MASt3R-SLAM geometry, linked over short intervals with SAM2 video
propagation, embedded once with SigLIP2, and jointly associated to persistent 3D
entities with visibility-conditioned unbalanced optimal transport (UOT). The
main extension is a reliability-gated, bounded multi-view appearance memory.
Appearance helps distinguish geometrically similar candidates, but cannot
bypass 3D spatial support, and uncertain assignments cannot update appearance
memory. At query time, visually neighbouring map entities automatically act as
hard negatives; Qwen3-VL is retained as the final verifier for a small ambiguous
shortlist. All formulae and thresholds below correspond to the implementation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and design objective}

At time $t$, a complete frame produces proposals
$\mathcal{P}_t=\{P_{ti}\}_{i=1}^{N_t}$. The persistent map before processing the
frame is $\mathcal{E}_{t-1}=\{E_j\}_{j=1}^{M_{t-1}}$. The number of proposals
varies from frame to frame, masks can fragment or merge, and many old entities
are outside the current camera view. The goal is therefore not a square
one-to-one assignment. We seek a partial, confidence-aware correspondence plus
explicit residual mass for births, fragments, misses, and occlusion.

The implemented design follows four constraints:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item all masks in a frame are compared jointly;
    \item geometry determines which proposal--entity pairs are possible;
    \item appearance is a soft identity cue whose influence is scaled by its
          measured reliability;
    \item semantic memory changes only after stricter confidence gates than
          those required for a geometric map update.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Pipeline}

For each finite video or HM3D sequence, the processing order is
\begin{equation}
\text{RGB}ightarrow\text{MASt3R-SLAM keyframes}ightarrow
\text{SAM2 masks}ightarrow\text{SAM2 tracklets}ightarrow
\text{SigLIP2 embeddings}ightarrow\text{UOT map}ightarrow
\text{searchable observation index}.
\end{equation}
The SigLIP2 image embeddings are computed before UOT, then reused after UOT by
attaching the final entity assignments. Thus image encoding is performed once,
not once for association and again for retrieval.

\section{Proposal construction}

Let $M_{ti}\subset\Omega_t$ be a cleaned SAM2 mask. MASt3R-SLAM supplies a
world-aligned point map $X_t^W(u)$ and geometry confidence for image pixel $u$.
The lifted proposal geometry is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{X}_{ti}=\{X_t^W(u):u\in M_{ti},\ u\text{ passes the geometry filter}\}.
\end{equation}
Each proposal stores its centroid $\bm{x}_{ti}$, 3D bounding box $B_{ti}$,
colours, optional MASt3R descriptors, original mask area $|M_{ti}|$, and the
number of retained world points $|\mathcal{X}_{ti}|$.

For short-term continuity, SAM2 video propagation yields a track identifier and
an adjacent-frame link IoU $q^{\mathrm{trk}}_{ti}$. This signal is only a soft
cue; it is not treated as a permanent identity.

\subsection{Masked SigLIP2 observation}

A contextual crop is taken around $M_{ti}$. Pixels inside the mask are retained,
while pixels outside the mask are moved towards neutral grey:
\begin{equation}
\widetilde I_{ti}(u)=
\begin{cases}
I_t(u), & u\in M_{ti},\\
\alpha I_t(u)+(1-\alpha)\,127, & u\notin M_{ti},
\end{cases}
\qquad \alpha=0.20.
\end{equation}
The crop includes a $15\%$ context margin by default. SigLIP2 produces a
unit-normalized appearance vector
\begin{equation}
\bm{z}_{ti}=\frac{f_{\mathrm{img}}(\widetilde I_{ti})}
{\|f_{\mathrm{img}}(\widetilde I_{ti})\|_2}.
\end{equation}

\section{Reliability of one appearance observation}

Four already available signals are converted to $[0,1]$:
\begin{align}
q^{\mathrm{sam}}_{ti} &= \clip(\text{SAM2 proposal score},0,1),\\
q^{\mathrm{lift}}_{ti} &= \min\left(1,
\frac{|\mathcal{X}_{ti}|}{\max(|M_{ti}|,1)}\right),\\
q^{\mathrm{trk}}_{ti} &=
\begin{cases}
\clip(\text{link IoU},0,1),&\text{if available},\\
0.75,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}\\
q^{\mathrm{res}}_{ti} &= \min\left(1,
\sqrt{\frac{|M_{ti}|}{4096}}\right).
\end{align}
The appearance reliability is their geometric mean,
\begin{equation}
r_{ti}=\left(q^{\mathrm{sam}}_{ti}
q^{\mathrm{lift}}_{ti}q^{\mathrm{trk}}_{ti}
q^{\mathrm{res}}_{ti}\right)^{1/4}.
\label{eq:reliability}
\end{equation}
The geometric mean is intentional: a high segmentation score cannot completely
hide a nearly empty 3D lift, a poor temporal link, or an extremely low-resolution
crop. It also avoids training a calibration network before labelled association
data exist.

\section{Persistent entity state}

Entity $E_j$ stores 3D geometry $\mathcal{Y}_j$, centroid $\bm{y}_j$, bounding
box $B_j$, colour and MASt3R statistics, observation count, timestamps, and a
bounded appearance memory
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{B}_j=\{(\bm{m}_{jk},r_{jk})\}_{k=1}^{K_j},
\qquad \|\bm{m}_{jk}\|_2=1,\qquad K_j\leq K_{\max}=8.
\end{equation}
Views are not averaged into one vector because front, side, distant, and
partially occluded observations can occupy different locations in the embedding
space.

For an admitted new view $(\bm z,r)$, let
$k^*=\arg\max_k\bm m_{jk}^{\top}\bm z$.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item If $\bm m_{jk^*}^{\top}\bm z\geq0.95$, the observation is redundant.
          It replaces view $k^*$ only when $r>r_{jk^*}$.
    \item If it is non-redundant and $K_j<8$, it is appended.
    \item If the bank is full, it replaces the least reliable stored view only
          when its reliability is higher.
\end{itemize}
This rule bounds memory, maintains view diversity, and prevents repeated weak
views from dominating an entity prototype.

\section{Proposal--entity cost}

\subsection{Strict spatial support}

Appearance is never allowed to connect arbitrary locations. A pair $(i,j)$ has
support only when
\begin{equation}
A_{ij}=\1\!\left[
\|\bm{x}_{ti}-\bm{y}_j\|_2\leq1.0\text{ m}
\quad\lor\quad
\operatorname{overlap}(B_{ti}^{+0.05},B_j^{+0.05})
\right].
\label{eq:support}
\end{equation}
Every pair with $A_{ij}=0$ receives infinite cost and exactly zero transport
mass.

\subsection{Geometric and visual cues}

For supported pairs, the existing normalized components are
\begin{align}
c^{\mathrm{cen}}_{ij}
&=\min\left(\frac{\|\bm{x}_{ti}-\bm{y}_j\|_2}{1.0\text{ m}},1\right),\\
c^{\mathrm{box}}_{ij}
&=1-\operatorname{IoU}_{3D}(B_{ti}^{+0.05},B_j^{+0.05}),\\
c^{\mathrm{geo}}_{ij}
&=1-\frac{1}{2}\left[
\frac{1}{|\widehat{\mathcal X}_{ti}|}
\sum_{\bm p\in\widehat{\mathcal X}_{ti}}
\1\!\left(\min_{\bm q\in\widehat{\mathcal Y}_j}
\|\bm p-\bm q\|_2\leq0.08\right)
\right.\\[-0.4em]
&\hspace{37mm}\left.+
\frac{1}{|\widehat{\mathcal Y}_j|}
\sum_{\bm q\in\widehat{\mathcal Y}_j}
\1\!\left(\min_{\bm p\in\widehat{\mathcal X}_{ti}}
\|\bm q-\bm p\|_2\leq0.08\right)
\right],
\end{align}
where each point set is deterministically sampled to at most 256 points.
Normalized colour distance and pooled MASt3R cosine distance provide
$c^{\mathrm{col}}_{ij}$ and $c^{\mathrm{desc}}_{ij}$ when available.

\subsection{Reliability-weighted soft best-view appearance}

Stored-view weights are
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{jk}=\frac{r_{jk}}{\sum_{\ell=1}^{K_j}r_{j\ell}}.
\end{equation}
Rather than compare against a mean embedding or use a discontinuous maximum,
the appearance similarity is a reliability-weighted smooth maximum,
\begin{equation}
s^{\mathrm{app}}_{ij}
=\tau\log\sum_{k=1}^{K_j}
\alpha_{jk}\exp\left(
\frac{\bm z_{ti}^{\top}\bm m_{jk}}{\tau}\right),
\qquad \tau=0.07,
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
c^{\mathrm{app}}_{ij}=\frac{1-\clip(s^{\mathrm{app}}_{ij},-1,1)}{2}.
\label{eq:appearance-cost}
\end{equation}
Its pair reliability is
\begin{equation}
\rho^{\mathrm{app}}_{ij}=
\sqrt{r_{ti}\left(\frac{1}{K_j}\sum_{k=1}^{K_j}r_{jk}\right)}.
\end{equation}
Thus poor evidence smoothly reduces the appearance weight instead of producing
an overconfident hard rejection.

If a SAM2 tracklet points to entity $j^*$ with confidence $h_i$, its temporal
cost is $c^{\mathrm{tmp}}_{ij}=\1[j\neq j^*]$ and its active weight is
$0.25h_i$. This cue also remains inside the spatial support in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:support}.

\subsection{Combined cost}

Let $\mathcal{L}_{ij}$ be the cues available for pair $(i,j)$. The final cost is
the active-weight normalized sum
\begin{equation}
C_{ij}=\frac{\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal L_{ij}}w^{ij}_{\ell}c^{\ell}_{ij}}
{\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal L_{ij}}w^{ij}_{\ell}},
\qquad A_{ij}=1.
\label{eq:combined-cost}
\end{equation}
The default base weights are shown in Table~\ref{tab:cost-weights}. Missing cues
are omitted rather than assigned a bad score.

\begin{table}[h]

\caption{Default pairwise cost weights.}
\label{tab:cost-weights}

Cue & centroid & box & geometry & colour & MASt3R & SigLIP & tracklet\\
\midrule

\section{Visibility-conditioned unbalanced transport}

\subsection{Proposal supply}

Proposal quality combines 3D retention and temporal support:
\begin{equation}
q_i=0.70q^{\mathrm{lift}}_{ti}+0.30q^{\mathrm{temp}}_{ti},
\qquad
q^{\mathrm{temp}}_{ti}=
\begin{cases}h_i,&\text{track hint exists},\\0.5,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
The proposal mass is $a_i=\max(q_i,10^{-3})$.

\subsection{Entity demand from visibility}

Up to 512 stored entity points are projected into the current calibrated view.
A projected point is visible if it lies in the image and its predicted depth is
not more than $0.15$ m behind the observed MASt3R depth. If $S_j$ points are
sampled and $V_j$ pass both tests,
\begin{equation}
v_j=\clip\left(\frac{V_j}{S_j},0,1\right),\qquad b_j=v_j.
\end{equation}
An active candidate column is floored at $10^{-3}$ for numerical stability.
Without intrinsics the explicit fallback is $v_j=1$.

\subsection{UOT objective}

The transport plan $\Pi\in\R_+^{N_t\times M_{t-1}}$ solves
\begin{align}
\min_{\Pi\geq0}\quad
&\langle \Pi,C\rangle
+\varepsilon\sum_{ij}\Pi_{ij}(\log\Pi_{ij}-1)\\
&+\sum_i\lambda_i\KL((\Pi\1)_i\,\|\,a_i)
+\sum_j\gamma_j\KL((\Pi^\top\1)_j\,\|\,b_j),\\
\text{subject to}\quad &\Pi_{ij}=0\quad\text{when }A_{ij}=0,
\label{eq:uot}
\end{align}
with $\varepsilon=0.05$ and scalar generalized KL
\begin{equation}
\KL(x\|y)=x\log(x/y)-x+y.
\end{equation}
Evidence controls how expensive it is to violate each marginal:
\begin{align}
\lambda_i&=0.05+q_i(0.50-0.05),\\
\gamma_j&=0.02+v_j(0.50-0.02).
\end{align}
A weak proposal may discard more mass, and an occluded or out-of-view entity
does not demand a match. This replaces a manually sized shared dustbin with
typed, evidence-dependent residuals.

\subsection{Generalized Sinkhorn updates}

On the active strict support, define $K_{ij}=\exp(-C_{ij}/\varepsilon)$ and zero
elsewhere. Let
\begin{equation}
\theta_i=\frac{\lambda_i}{\lambda_i+\varepsilon},\qquad
\phi_j=\frac{\gamma_j}{\gamma_j+\varepsilon}.
\end{equation}
Starting with $\bm u=\1$ and $\bm v=\1$, the implemented fixed-point updates
are
\begin{align}
u_i&\leftarrow\left(\frac{a_i}{(K\bm v)_i}\right)^{\theta_i},\\
v_j&\leftarrow\left(\frac{b_j}{(K^\top\bm u)_j}\right)^{\phi_j},\\
\Pi&=\operatorname{diag}(\bm u)K\operatorname{diag}(\bm v).
\end{align}
They are evaluated in the log domain, for at most 2000 iterations, until the
maximum scaling change is below $10^{-7}$.

\subsection{Residual interpretation and hard decisions}

The proposal birth/noise residual and entity miss/occlusion residual are
\begin{align}
\beta_i&=\pospart{a_i-\sum_j\Pi_{ij}},\\
\mu_j&=\pospart{b_j-\sum_i\Pi_{ij}}.
\end{align}
Excess mass in either direction is stored separately rather than silently
clipped. For proposal $i$, define
\begin{equation}
R_i=\frac{\sum_j\Pi_{ij}}{a_i},\qquad
p_{ij}=\frac{\Pi_{ij}}{\sum_k\Pi_{ik}}.
\end{equation}
A proposal becomes a match only if:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item at least one supported candidate has $C_{ij}\leq0.65$;
    \item $R_i\geq0.25$;
    \item for the maximum-mass viable candidate, $p_{ij}\geq0.50$.
\end{enumerate}
Otherwise its reason is recorded as empty map, no spatial candidate, cost above
threshold, low retained mass, or ambiguous transport.

\section{Delayed entity birth}

An unmatched mask does not immediately become a persistent entity. Residual
evidence is accumulated under its SAM2 track ID. A new entity is committed only
when all default conditions hold:
\begin{align}
n_{\mathrm{obs}}&\geq3,\\
\frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{obs}}}\sum_s\frac{\beta_s}{a_s}&\geq0.55,\\
\operatorname{median}_s(\operatorname{IoU}^{\mathrm{link}}_s)&\geq0.60,\\
\max_s\|\bm x_s-\bm x_{s-1}\|_2&\leq0.30\text{ m}.
\end{align}
Single-frame fragments expire by default. Once a track is committed, a temporary
UOT rejection cannot create a duplicate entity with the same track ID.

\section{Confidence-gated appearance-memory update}

Geometric state and semantic appearance state use different update thresholds.
For a matched pair $(i,j)$, the new appearance view is eligible only if
\begin{equation}
r_{ti}\geq0.45,\qquad p_{ij}\geq0.70,\qquad R_i\geq0.50,
\label{eq:memory-gate}
\end{equation}
and, when a link IoU exists,
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{IoU}^{\mathrm{link}}_{ti}\geq0.60.
\end{equation}
It must then pass the diverse-bank rule in Section 5. A match may therefore
update 3D geometry without updating appearance memory. This separation is the
main protection against confirmation feedback: a weak semantic match is not
allowed to make its own future appearance score stronger.

Every decision is saved with its reliability, conditional probability, retained
ratio, track IoU, update flag, and blocking reasons. The entity artifact stores
its appearance bank, per-view reliabilities, view count, and mean reliability.

\section{Open-vocabulary retrieval without manual confounders}

After mapping, the pre-UOT embedding matrix is reused unchanged and the final
entity/track assignments are attached. Let $\bm e_n$ be one normalized stored
observation embedding. Four prompt templates are embedded for query text $q$,
and their mean similarity gives
\begin{equation}
s_n^+(q)=\frac{1}{L}\sum_{\ell=1}^{L}
\bm e_n^\top\bm t_{\ell}(q).
\end{equation}

\subsection{Map-derived hard negatives}

Each confirmed entity has a normalized map prototype
\begin{equation}
\bm p_j=\frac{\sum_{n\in E_j}\bm e_n}
{\left\|\sum_{n\in E_j}\bm e_n\right\|_2}.
\end{equation}
For entity $j$, choose the three other entities with largest
$\bm p_j^\top\bm p_k$; call this set $\mathcal N_3(j)$. These are the objects the
current map itself says are visually confusable. With normalized mean query
embedding $\overline{\bm t}(q)$, the automatic hard-negative score is
\begin{equation}
h_j(q)=\max_{k\in\mathcal N_3(j)}
\overline{\bm t}(q)^\top\bm p_k.
\label{eq:map-negative}
\end{equation}
No object-specific list such as ``clock versus picture frame'' is required.
Generic background/fragment text negatives remain a safety floor. For view $n$
of entity $j$,
\begin{align}
s_n^-(q)&=\max\left(
\max_g\bm e_n^\top\bm t_g^-,\ h_j(q)\right),\\
m_n(q)&=s_n^+(q)-s_n^-(q).
\end{align}

The view quality used during aggregation is
\begin{equation}
\omega_n=q_n^{\mathrm{sam}}q_n^{\mathrm{assoc}}
\min\left(1,\sqrt{\frac{|M_n|}{4096}}\right).
\end{equation}
The entity score is the $\omega_n$-weighted mean of its best three supporting
view margins. A fast retrieval requires at least two views with
$m_n\geq0.02$ and entity margin at least $0.02$. This particularly suppresses
small-object false positives that appear plausible in only one frame.

\subsection{Qwen3-VL final verification}

For difficult small objects, only a small SigLIP-ranked set of confirmed
persistent entities is sent to Qwen3-VL. Each candidate is represented by up to
three highlighted views, and several candidates are verified listwise in one
generation. The verifier is instructed to judge only highlighted pixels. A
candidate is accepted only when the structured verdict is ``yes'', confidence
is at least $0.75$, and at least two supplied frames support it. Invalid or
missing structured output fails closed. Verdicts are cached by query, model,
entity ID, prompt version, and evidence-image bytes.

Qwen3-VL cannot alter geometric identities; it only verifies the final semantic
retrieval shortlist. This keeps the common query path fast while reserving the
large model for ambiguity.

\section{What changed and why}

The implementation makes six concrete changes to the earlier geometry-only
delayed-UOT mapper:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Pre-UOT SigLIP2 encoding.} Appearance is available during
          association and the expensive image pass is reused for retrieval.
    \item \textbf{Reliability-scaled appearance cost.} Appearance resolves
          nearby repeated geometry, but weak masks contribute little and cannot
          create non-spatial matches.
    \item \textbf{Bounded diverse entity memory.} Eight explicit views preserve
          viewpoint variation without unbounded storage or destructive averaging.
    \item \textbf{Confidence-gated updates.} Stricter semantic-memory gates break
          the self-reinforcing error loop caused by uncertain assignments.
    \item \textbf{Map-derived negatives.} The map automatically supplies the
          closest visual competitors for every query, removing the need to type
          object-specific confounders.
    \item \textbf{Small final VLM stage.} Qwen3-VL checks highlighted multi-view
          evidence only after fast retrieval, rather than scanning every mask.
\end{enumerate}

The strongest research claim should be the interaction of visibility-conditioned
residual transport, reliability-scaled multi-view identity memory, and separate
memory-update confidence---not the independent use of MASt3R, SAM2, SigLIP2, or
Qwen3-VL.

\section{Required evaluation}

The method should be reported through controlled ablations:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item geometry-only delayed UOT;
    \item $+$ unweighted single-vector appearance;
    \item $+$ reliability-scaled eight-view appearance;
    \item $+$ confidence-gated memory updates;
    \item retrieval with generic negatives versus map-derived negatives;
    \item fast retrieval versus final Qwen3-VL verification.
\end{enumerate}
Mapping metrics should include identity switches, entity over-fragmentation,
duplicate births, unmatched reasons, and memory-update rejection reasons.
Retrieval metrics should include entity-level precision/recall, small-object false
positives, supporting-view count, query time, and VLM cache hit rate. The fixed
thresholds above are initial defaults and should be tuned only on a validation
split, never on the final evaluation scenes.

\section{Limitations}

The current method assumes a mostly static scene and sufficiently aligned
MASt3R-SLAM geometry. SigLIP2 is not trained specifically for tiny masked indoor
parts, so very small or visually featureless objects may remain ambiguous.
Map-derived negatives require at least two confirmed entities and can suppress a
true result when two physical instances are nearly identical; the final
multi-view verifier is intended to handle that case. Appearance does not repair
a wrong 3D spatial gate, and Qwen3-VL adds substantial model-load latency on an
uncached query. These are measurable limitations rather than hidden failure
modes because the pipeline saves transport, reliability, update, and verification
diagnostics for audit.
